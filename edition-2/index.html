This is not a system for arriving at a final doctrine. It is a bounded inquiry: biology describes conditions without declaring soul-purpose; theological traditions are treated in their own terms; and AI is approached as a powerful tool whose ethical significance still requires human answerability.

Chapter 1 · Embodiment

Embodiment Is a Condition, Not an Explanation

Finite bodies, ageing, energy, attention, and the distinction between biological mechanism and ultimate meaning.

 Chapter 2 · Witness

Witness Beyond the Argument

Recognition, gratitude, silence, and the place where a lived question exceeds instrumental explanation.

 Chapter 3 · Action

Action After Acquisition

Work and responsibility after compulsive self-proof loses its authority over the purpose of action.

 Chapter 4 · Inquiry

Inquiry, Intelligence, and the Tool That Cannot Witness

Generative AI, lived responsibility, education, and the epistemic boundary between output and experience.

 Chapter 5 · Comparative Method

Words at the Boundary: Iqra, Shema, Bodhi, Jago, and Awaken

A methodological appendix for reading resonances without erasing the distinct worlds from which words arise.
