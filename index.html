Agency, responsibility, and system design in the age of AI.

This is not a collection of blog posts. It is a living work built from public essays, DOI-linked research notes, project evidence, and documented systems. Its central question is simple: when a system becomes powerful enough to act, how can it remain accountable to the human purpose it was built to serve?
