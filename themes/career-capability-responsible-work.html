Career Capability & Responsible Work

A reading path for questions about learning, capability, agency, meaningful work, career reflection, and responsibility after acquisition.

Reading boundary

These chapters are reflective and educational. They are not individual employment, financial, or clinical advice, and they do not promise job outcomes.

Questions this pathway holds open

  • What is the difference between information, skill, capability, and agency?
  • How can work remain responsible after self-proof or external status stops being the only measure?
