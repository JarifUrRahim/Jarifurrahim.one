Responsible AI, Human Oversight & Agency

A reading path for questions about AI output, human judgment, permissions, responsibility, and the limits of automation.

Reading boundary

These chapters explore accountable use and human answerability. They are not a legal-compliance manual, safety certification, or claim that AI possesses lived responsibility.

Questions this pathway holds open

  • How can AI be used without surrendering judgment or responsibility?
  • What should remain under human review when systems become capable of acting?
